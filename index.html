About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. My research focuses on the security and construction of lattice-based proxy re-encryption, with broader interests in lattice-based cryptography and provable security.

Research Interests

  • Proxy re-encryption
  • Lattice-based cryptography
  • Post-quantum cryptography
  • Provable security
